NoAdReader

A quiet ebook reader for Android. It reads the books you already have on your phone, with no ads, no account and no internet access.

Contact us

Coming to Google Play. The store link will be added here once the app is published.

What it does

  • Your library: pick the folders where your books live and NoAdReader builds the library for you.
  • Formats: EPUB and PDF, plus DRM-free MOBI, AZW3, TXT, FB2 and CBZ comics.
  • Comfortable reading: light, sepia, dark and black themes, fonts, text size, spacing and margins.
  • Highlights and notes: five colours, searchable in one place, exported as Markdown.
  • Read aloud: uses the voices already on your phone.
  • Reading stats and widgets: time read, streaks, a daily goal, and home-screen widgets.
  • Backups you control: daily local backups and a full export to a place you choose.

Privacy by design

The app does not ask Android for the internet permission, so it cannot go online. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your books are never copied or uploaded. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself.

Crash reports are off by default; if you turn them on, nothing is sent automatically. Read the full privacy policy.

Optional tips

The app has no paywall and nothing is locked. If you like it, you can leave a small tip through Google Play. Ribbon, the app's cat, will be pleased.
