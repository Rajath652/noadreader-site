Privacy policy

NoAdReader for Android, published by RLabs, KR Puram, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560036, India. Effective date: 30 October 2026. Hosted at https://noadreader.com/privacy. Draft: have a lawyer review before relying on it.

Short version: the app works offline, has no account, no ads and no trackers, and nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself.

What the app does not do

  • No network access. The app does not ask Android for the INTERNET permission, so the system itself stops it from going online. It also does not ask for network-state access. A build check fails if any library tries to add either permission.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no login.
  • No ads, analytics or trackers. No advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking libraries are included. We aim for an Exodus Privacy report showing zero trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We never receive your data, so there is nothing to sell or share.

Where your data is kept

Everything stays on your phone:

DataWhere
Your booksIn the folders you choose. The app reads them; it never copies, changes or uploads them. TXT, FB2 and CBZ files are converted to EPUB in the app's private cache to open them.
Library, reading positions, highlights, notes, bookmarks, collections, reading statsA database in the app's private storage
SettingsThe app's private storage
CoversThe app's private storage
Daily backups (the last 7)The app's private storage

Other apps cannot read the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it, so use Settings > Export my data first if you want to keep a copy. Export writes a .zip file only to the place you pick.

If Android's own device backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's database (library, reading positions, highlights, notes, bookmarks, collections, stats) and settings in that backup, and in a transfer to a new phone. Converted books, covers and the app's daily backups are left out. That backup is run by Android and your Google account, not by us.

Features that hand data to other apps, only when you ask

  • Look up and Translate pass the words you selected to the dictionary or translate app you choose. That app follows its own privacy policy.
  • Share and Export send exactly what you chose to the app or place you pick.
  • Read aloud uses the text-to-speech voices installed on your phone. Voices that need the network are hidden.

Optional tips

The app is free. If you choose to leave a tip, Google Play handles the entire purchase, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The app receives no payment data (no card, name, email or account details) and collects nothing about you. The only thing it keeps is a small flag in its private storage on your phone, so it can remember that you tipped or asked not to be asked again. That flag is left out of Android's device backup. The tip screen's "Maybe later" and "Don't ask again" choices work entirely on your phone.

Crash reports (opt-in)

Crash reports are off by default. If you turn on Settings > Send crash reports:

  1. After a crash the app asks whether you want to send a report. You can say no.
  2. If you say yes, your phone's share menu opens with a short text report. You pick the app (for example email) and can read or edit the report before sending it.
  3. Nothing is ever sent automatically, and the app has no server to send it to.

The report contains only: a random report ID, the app version, the Android version, the phone brand and model, the time of the crash, your optional comment and a scrubbed stack trace. Before the report is shown, the app removes every error message (which can hold file names or content:// addresses) and replaces anything that looks like an address or a file path with a placeholder, so only the names of the error types and the app's code locations remain. It never includes your books, notes, highlights, file names or paths, file lists, logs, device IDs or settings. The one thing you control is the optional comment: it is included exactly as you type it, so leave out personal details. Crash reports use the open-source ACRA library (Apache 2.0) with its upload feature left out.

Sync (future)

Sync is not built yet. When it is added, it will be off until you turn it on and will store your data with a provider you choose (such as your own cloud drive or WebDAV server). Turning it on will be the only thing that lets the app go online, and this policy will be updated before that release.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Folder access that you grant through Android's folder picker, to read your books.
  • Notifications and foreground service, to show read-aloud playback controls.
  • Run at startup and wake lock, for the optional daily library rescan and the daily local backup.

Age and children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. It collects no personal data from anyone.
